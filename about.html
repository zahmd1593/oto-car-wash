WHO WE ARE

WE LIKE CARS
LOOKING THEIR BEST.

OTO Car Wash is a local car care business serving Fredericton, New Brunswick.

Our goal is to make taking care of your vehicle simple. Instead of filling our menu with dozens of confusing packages, we focus on three important services: interior detailing, exterior detailing and oil changes.

Whether your interior needs a refresh, your exterior needs its shine back, or it's simply time for routine maintenance, OTO is here to help.
